# Immutable Script Revision Pinning and Per-Post Comment Energy Cap

> Amendment 2026-10-29: Every published script edit creates a new revision. Repeat comments are free and award no relationship. See [closed-beta MVP decisions](../froststory-beta-mvp.md). The original text below records the earlier decision.


Status: accepted

In the Social Story Platform, active player DM threads pin to the published `Script Revision` they started on (allowing non-breaking text patches in-place while isolating structural choice/condition edits into a new revision), and each player can spend `Energy Token` to comment and gain `+1` relationship at most once per post.

## Why

- Prevents in-flight player chat state corruption when the solo creator updates branching dialogue or unlock conditions in Creator & Content Studio.
- Prevents players from farming unlimited relationship points on a single post by spamming repeated comments, tying relationship progression directly to content cadence.
